06 — TRUST & GOVERNANCE

AI should know what it knows.

Sentinel Grid prevents probabilistic AI inference from silently becoming assumed fact. Every output is strictly typed with an authoritative truth status before reaching officer screens.

OBSERVED
Direct Ground Truth
Verified hardware telemetry, physical RTSP stream state, network pings, and logged GIS coordinates.
INFERRED
Model Predictions
YOLOv8 bounding boxes, vehicle classifications, and OCR character extractions accompanied by confidence scores.
UNCERTAIN
Consensus Flag
Detections falling below temporal multi-frame confidence thresholds, routed to human officer review queue.
NOT_AVAILABLE
Zero Hallucination
Information not present in monitored feeds is explicitly returned as unavailable rather than fabricated.
NOT_READABLE
Optical Fault
Optical glare, low resolution, motion blur, or physical camera obstruction preventing deterministic OCR.
